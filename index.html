📋 Market Brief
The Humanisation of Pets
Pet owners globally now spend on their animals what prior generations spent on children. Premiumisation of food, healthcare, grooming and even psychology is the defining macro trend. In the US, 67% of households own a pet. India is accelerating — urban pet ownership grew 30% post-COVID.
APPA 2023–24 Survey · ET Pet Economy 2023
📈 Trend Watch
Post-COVID Pet Boom Is Permanent
COVID adoption surge created 10M+ new pet households globally. Unlike prior boom-bust cycles, vet visits, premium food spend, and subscriptions maintained trajectory post-lockdown. India saw 15% YoY growth in vet clinic footfall and a 42% jump in online pet supplies in 2022.
Dogster Industry Report 2023
💹 Investment Signal
VC Capital Pouring In
Global pet tech raised $3.4B in 2022. Key verticals: food-tech ($900M), telehealth ($680M), insurance ($550M), GPS/IoT ($420M). India saw 12 pet-sector funding rounds in 2022–23. Supertails raised $10M, Wiggles $5M, HUFT crossed ₹600 Cr GMV.
PitchBook 2023 · Inc42 India Pet Tracker
🔬 Research Note
Millennials & Gen Z Driving Everything
71% of Indian pet owners are under 35. They delay marriage, delay kids, but adopt pets. They demand transparent food ingredients, prefer subscriptions, and trust Instagram vets over neighbourhood clinics. This cohort is the entire market.
YouGov India 2023 · Kantar Millennial Pet Report
🔭 Sector Watch
6 Sectors. 6 Opportunity Pools.
Pet Food (45%), Vet Services (28%), Accessories (9%), Grooming (8%), Training (4%), Tech/Platform (6%). Tech is smallest but growing fastest. Food is dominated by multinationals — but India's homegrown brands are taking back share.
IMARC India Pet Market Report 2023
⚖️ Policy Watch
Regulation Is Waking Up
India's ABC Rules 2001 revised in 2023. Pet registration mandatory in 3 states. Microchipping passed as mandate in Maharashtra. PCA Act amendments tabled in Parliament. Infrastructure is lagging — direct opportunity for tech.
MoFAHD 2023 · Maharashtra Gazette

🌍 Global Scale
The US Is the Benchmark
Americans spent $147B on pets in 2023 — up from $136B in 2022. 67% of US households own a pet. The US market is mature but still growing at 5–6% CAGR. It sets the product, service, and technology standard every other market eventually follows.
APPA National Pet Owners Survey 2023–24
🌏 Asia Pacific
Asia Is Where Growth Lives
Asia-Pacific pet care is growing at 9–11% CAGR — the fastest of any region. China reached $28B in 2023. South Korea: 31% pet ownership penetration. Japan's pet market is larger than its baby care market. India is the youngest and fastest accelerating.
Mordor Intelligence 2023 · Euromonitor Asia
🔬 Mandate Wave
10 of Top 15 Markets Already Mandate Microchipping
UK, Australia, all 27 EU states (from 2025), New Zealand, Germany, France — mandatory microchipping is the norm in developed markets. India is pre-mandate. Maharashtra passed it in 2023. National mandate is a matter of when, not if.
EU Parliament June 2025
📊 Food Dominance
Mars & Nestlé Own 40% of Global Pet Food
Mars Petcare ($21B) and Nestlé Purina ($17B) together control ~40% of the global pet food market. Below them: Hill's, Royal Canin, Spectrum Brands. The food category is consolidated. The platform/tech layer has no such consolidation — it remains fragmented and open.
Mars Petcare Annual Report · Nestlé Purina AR
💊 Health Tech
Pet Health Tech Is the New Frontier
Zoetis hit $8.5B revenue in 2023. IDEXX Laboratories crossed $3.6B. Telehealth platforms Pawp, Fuzzy, Dutch serve 3M+ US pet owners. Wearables track vitals. India has none of this at scale — yet.
Zoetis AR 2023 · IDEXX AR 2023
🇮🇳 India Position
India: Ranked 5th, Growing Fastest
India is the 5th largest pet market by animal population, 8th by spend. At 17–20% CAGR it will enter the top 5 by spend within this decade. No other top-15 market is growing this fast. The window to build foundational infrastructure is open now.
IMARC Group 2023 · Grand View Research

🏙️ Urban Concentration
10 Cities, 70% of the Market
Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune, Kolkata, Ahmedabad, Chandigarh, and Kochi account for ~70% of formal pet spending. Tier 2 India has almost none of this — and is growing faster.

🐕 What India Has
Real, Growing Players. No Platform Layer.
India has well-funded e-commerce (Supertails, Wiggles), premium retail (HUFT), domestic food (Drools, Pedigree India), vet chains (Vetic, Cessna), and a growing grooming segment. Each player owns a slice. None owns the identity layer that connects them all.

🐾 The Playful Side & The Sad Side
The market is two worlds simultaneously. One is adorable and growing fast. The other is a public health emergency hiding in plain sight.

🎀 The Playful Side
Pet Fashion Is a ₹400 Cr Industry
Dog clothes, luxury collars, paw balms, nail polish, even pet jewellery — the Indian premium accessories segment crossed ₹400 Cr in 2023. HUFT charges ₹2,500 for a single dog sweater and sells out. Pet photoshoots, pet cafés, pet spas. The joy economy is very real.
HUFT Annual Report 2023 · ET Retail
📸 The Playful Side
Pet Influencers Earn More Than Humans
India's top pet Instagram accounts earn ₹50,000–₹2L per post. Brands like Drools, PetSutra, and HUFT spend 40% of marketing on influencer collaborations. The pet creator economy in India is estimated at ₹80–120 Cr annually, growing at 90% YoY.
Influencer Marketing Hub India 2023
💻 The Playful Side
Pet Telehealth Normalised Post-COVID
Online vet consultations grew 300% in India 2020–2023. Apps like Wiggles, Supertails, and PetKonnect offer 24/7 chat with registered vets. Average Indian urban pet owner now does 1.2 digital vet interactions per year.
Supertails Growth Report 2023
🎮 The Playful Side
The Pet Food Premiumisation Wave
Grain-free, raw, freeze-dried, breed-specific — Indian pet food is going through the same premiumisation curve the US went through in the 2000s. Drools crossed ₹500 Cr revenue. Farmina and Orijen sell out within days of restocking in Bengaluru.
Drools Annual Report 2023
⚠️ The Sad Side
62 Million Stray Dogs. No System.
India has the world's largest stray dog population — 62M per WHO. The Animal Birth Control programme is underfunded, poorly tracked, and varies wildly by city. No unified national database exists. Bengaluru had 40,000 registered strays in 2022; actual count estimated at 3× that.
WHO Rabies Fact Sheet 2024 · State of Pet Homelessness Index
⚠️ The Sad Side
5M+ Dog Bites Per Year. 20,000 Deaths.
India accounts for 36% of global rabies deaths. 5.4M dog bites recorded annually — real figure 3–4× higher. Most victims are children under 15. Direct economic cost: ₹1,200 Cr per year in treatment and lost workdays.
MoHFW 2022 · WHO Rabies Bulletin
⚠️ The Sad Side
Abandonments Spike 40% Post-COVID
Pandemic adoptions came with no support system. When owners returned to office, thousands of pets were abandoned. Shelters across Bengaluru, Mumbai, and Delhi reported 40% capacity overrun in 2022. Most rescue organisations have zero digital infrastructure.
Humane World for Animals India 2023
⚠️ The Sad Side
Vet Desert in Tier 2 India
India has 1 vet per 17,000 animals — WHO recommends 1 per 5,000. 80% of registered vets work in large-animal agriculture. A pet owner in Coimbatore or Guwahati has almost no access to emergency care.
WHO Vet Infrastructure Report · Veterinary Council of India
⚠️ The Sad Side
Rescue Orgs Running on WhatsApp
India has 3,000+ registered animal rescue organisations. Nearly all manage intake, volunteer coordination, foster matching, and medical records through WhatsApp groups and paper registers. No unified platform. Cases fall through the cracks. This is not a funding problem — it is an infrastructure problem.
People for Animals India Survey 2022 · IPICA Research 2023
