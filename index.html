What we're building & why

aaawoo is a pet platform for India — built so that pet identity, services, community, and safety live in one place.

The brand architecture is deliberate: the company sits back, the mascots do the talking. aaawoo doesn't lead with its name, its founders, or its features — it leads with a mascot universe.
